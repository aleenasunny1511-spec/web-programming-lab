ICET

Established in 2002 by the Ilahia Trust, ICET stands as one of Kerala's pioneering self-financing engineering colleges. The institution is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and is affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). Set across a sprawling green campus, the college offers a variety of undergraduate (B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.Tech, MCA, MBA) programs spanning traditional and emerging technological disciplines. Driven by its motto of "Dynamism, Growth and Selfless Service," the college emphasizes academic brilliance, technical research, social commitment through various scholarship schemes, and robust career placement opportunities supported by prominent corporate recruiters
